HOW TO JOIN

STEP 1

Join & verify

Join the Discord and link your Roblox account with RoVer in the verification channel.

STEP 2

Register

Type /register. You start at 200 Elo in Iron, and the ranked channels open.

STEP 3

Find a match

Type /find. The bot pairs you with a player at your level. One duel on Arena, knife damage only.
