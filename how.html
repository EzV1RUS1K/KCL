HOW TO PLAY

STEP 1

Join & verify

Join the ARQ Discord and link your Roblox account with RoVer in the verification channel.

STEP 2

Register

Type /register. You start at 200 Elo in Iron. Your Discord and Roblox accounts must be at least 30 days old.

STEP 3

Find a match

Type /find. The bot finds an opponent at your level and opens a private match thread.

Join Discord

The match

  • 1v1 duel in Rivals on Arena. One duel = one match.
  • Damage with the knife only. Guns are not allowed.
  • Your loadout (utility etc.) is agreed with your opponent before the duel.
  • After the duel press I won or I lost in the match thread. "I won" must be confirmed by your opponent.
  • Don't agree? Press Dispute and post a clip. Staff decide.

Ranked and friendly

  • /find — ranked: the bot picks your opponent, Elo changes. Max 2 ranked matches per day vs the same player.
  • /challenge @player — friendly: no Elo, nothing saved. The challenge lasts 30 seconds.

How Elo works

  • Everyone starts at 200. Win → you gain Elo, lose → you lose Elo.
  • Beat a stronger player → more points. Beat a weaker one → fewer. An equal win is about +8.
  • Your first 10 matches count more, so you reach your real level faster.
  • You appear on the leaderboard after 5 ranked matches. Elo never goes below 0.

Ranks

  • Rusty 0–199 · Iron 200–399 · Steel 400–599 · Damascus 600–799 · Obsidian 800–999 · Bladelord 1000+

Fair play

  • No cheats, scripts, macros or alt accounts. One Roblox account = one player.
  • 3 active warnings → 3-day ranked ban, 5 → permanent. Warnings expire after 30 days.
  • Report players with /report in Discord. Reports are private.
